# Privacy Policy — MaTrIX Co Gaming Browser

Short version: this browser collects nothing and sends nothing to the developer. Your data stays on your device.

## What stays on your device
- Bookmarks, history, open tabs, settings, and downloaded filter lists.
- Your AI API key, stored encrypted (Android Keystore). App backup is disabled, so Android never copies this data anywhere.

## What leaves your device, only when you use the feature
- **AI page features:** only when you tap one, the current page's text is sent to the Anthropic API, using your own API key. The cost and Anthropic's data terms are between you and Anthropic.
- **Prayer times:** the address you type, or your approximate coordinates, are sent to the Aladhan API (api.aladhan.com).
- **QR codes:** the page URL is opened at api.qrserver.com to render the code.
- **Ad and tracker blocking:** filter lists are downloaded from their public hosts (EasyList and similar). Those hosts see your IP address, like any download.
- **Updates:** the app checks GitHub Releases for a newer version. No device information is sent.

## What the app never does
- No account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDK.
- No collection, sale, or sharing of personal data with anyone.
